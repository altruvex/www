Left: today — Outfit display + Vazirmatn Arabic, exactly as shipped. Right: Altruvex Sans (harmonised) — one family, two script files. Latin is Outfit, renamed, outlines untouched (the letters are identical on both sides — the signature glyphs come later, from a type designer; what differs in English is tracking, below). Arabic is Vazirmatn with its Latin removed and its outlines, advances and mark anchors scaled ×1.0625 inside the font, so the alef lands exactly on Outfit's ascender (0.714 em) with no CSS scaling; Arabic keeps its own deep ascent/descent so gradient accents still paint the dots below the line. ×1.0625 is the chosen scale (2026-09-27). Inter body and Geist Mono labels are unchanged. Weights as live: hero EN 300, hero AR 600, titles 600; Arabic headings line-height 1.3. Latin tracking ladder — the right column's English display type takes its letter-spacing from its own size (section 05), instead of one fixed value per element; Arabic stays at 0 at every size. Switch it to Live to see the fonts alone. Accent gradients are simplified to iris — this page tests type, not colour. F flips in Flip view.
