Altruvex Sans stress test

Prototype · 2026-09-27. Every specimen is set in Altruvex Sans (Latin proto + Arabic ×1.0625 proto, the same files as compare.html); labels are Geist Mono. Default weight 400 unless a section says otherwise; the weight menu overrides the default only. Targets (em): Latin ascender 0.714 · cap 0.694 · x-height 0.475 · descender −0.199 · Arabic alef 0.714 · tooth 0.317. Arabic letter-spacing is always 0. Latin tracking ladder: 0 below 20px, −0.010@20 → −0.040@136 at weight 400, log-interpolated, clamped above 136, then scaled per weight by the spacing factor k (300 ×1.074 · 500 ×0.918 · 600 ×0.865 · 700 ×0.803 · 800 ×0.75). This page records evidence only; notes say what to look at, not what to conclude.

01 · Metric alignment

What to look for: where the tops of h/d/l, capitals and x-height letters sit against their guides; where alef tops and tooth tops sit; where Arabic dots and marks land relative to the shaded zones; whether Latin and Arabic in one string share a common visual height. 120px, Latin at ladder tracking. Faint grey behind = today's Outfit / Vazirmatn at the same size and origin.

baseline 0x-height 0.475 · cap 0.694 · ascender 0.714 · descender −0.199 tooth 0.317 · alef 0.714 (dashed)dots/marks zones ≈0.714–0.95 and 0 to −0.40today's fonts (ghost)

02 · Arabic glyph stress

What to look for: join continuity between forms, stroke weight consistency across letters, tooth heights against the tooth line, bowl and tail depths below the baseline, dot placement and size. Forms built with ZWJ (U+200D). Guide lines: baseline measured per cell from a zero-size inline marker; tooth drawn at baseline − 0.317em (approximate: it is a background line, not a font metric readout).

03 · Dots & diacritics

What to look for: marks clipped by the line box, marks colliding with dots or with the line above/below, shadda + vowel stacking, marks centred over the correct letter. Dashed = block line box; dotted = inline content area (the font's own ascent/descent).

Vocalised strings stacked · 32px · stripes = alternating line boxes

04 · Mixed RTL / LTR

What to look for: where punctuation, periods, dashes, "+", "$" and digits end up in each base direction; whether runs keep their internal order; spacing at script boundaries. Each string rendered with dir="ltr", dir="rtl" and dir="auto" at 24px, letter-spacing 0. The mono caption is the logical (stored) order, left to right, one codepoint per box, coloured by approximate bidi class (R · L · underlined = digits EN/AN · grey = neutral / marks).

05 · Numerals

What to look for: digit widths and heights, whether digits sit on the baseline with the letters around them, separators (, . ٬ ٫) spacing, how proportional and tabular widths differ, and whether the price columns line up on the units digit. letter-spacing 0 here.

Column alignment · 48px · right-aligned (rule = the right edge)

06 · Punctuation

What to look for: vertical placement of each mark against the baseline line, weight match with letters, and the space each mark leaves on either side (sidebearings) between Latin letters (n?n) and between Arabic letters (ب؟ب). letter-spacing 0 here.

07 · Weights

What to look for: whether each weight step looks like an even step in both scripts, whether Arabic and Latin at the same number look the same darkness, counters closing up at 700, hairlines disappearing at 300. Fixed weights (the weight menu does not apply). Latin at ladder tracking.

08 · Sizes

What to look for: spacing that feels loose at large sizes or tight at small ones, Arabic vs Latin optical size at each step, detail loss at 12–14px. Weight from the menu (default 400). The label shows the letter-spacing applied to Latin.

tracking

09 · Tracking ladder

What to look for: at each size, the value where letters stop looking loose and before they start touching (r-n, t-e, "ng"). The row with a blue rule is the ladder's own value for that size (inserted when it is not in the list). Weight from the menu.

10 · Word silhouettes

What to look for: whether the blurred shape of each word is distinct and evenly textured (no dark knots or holes), and whether Arabic and Latin blurs carry a similar density. 64px; normal copy then the same word blurred 4px. Latin at ladder tracking.

11 · Long-form

What to look for: evenness of texture over a paragraph, rivers, how digits and Latin sit inside Arabic running text and vice versa, rhythm at each size. Test text written for this specimen (not site copy). EN line-height 1.6, max 68ch; AR line-height 1.8 (the brief's value for the live site's Arabic body leading, not re-measured here), max 60ch. letter-spacing 0.

12 · Line heights

What to look for: ascenders/marks touching the line above, descenders/dots touching the line below, the leading where Arabic and mixed paragraphs stop colliding. 18px, max 40ch. Alternating stripes are 1lh tall, so each stripe is one line box.

13 · UI

What to look for: legibility at 13–16px, vertical centring of labels in buttons, badges and tabs, digit alignment in the table, mixed-direction dates and times. All text in Altruvex Sans at the menu weight; Arabic table headers are this page's own translation (see note).

What to look for: hero and section titles at each width, line breaks, single-word last lines, overflow. Each frame is laid out at its real width and scaled to fit this column.
