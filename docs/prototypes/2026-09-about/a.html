The studio

Altruvex is a small studio in Cairo that builds multilingual web systems for businesses that need architecture, not a template with a brand coat.

There is no sales team, no account manager and no subcontractor. The person on your first call is the person who writes the code, and still the person on the call at launch.

02

The fine print

  1. i

    We argue you out of scope.

    If a page, a feature or a phase will not earn back what it costs, we say so before it is quoted.

    What it costs us

    The recommendation that removes a feature removes a line from our own invoice.

  2. ii

    The number does not move quietly.

    Estimates are broken down before the contract. A change order arrives before the work starts, not with the final invoice.

    What it costs us

    A number fixed in advance is a bet against our own estimate, and we pay when it is wrong.

  3. iii

    It becomes yours at final payment.

    Repositories, domains, accounts and pipelines are issued in your name. Nothing is engineered to make leaving difficult.

    What it costs us

    We keep no leverage over a client who decides to walk.

  4. iv

    We build for the engineer after us.

    Documented architecture, conventional patterns. The handover assumes we will not be here.

    What it costs us

    A system anyone can read is a system anyone can be hired to continue.
