About Altruvex

Four letters we hold ourselves to.

Altruvex is a small studio in Cairo building multilingual web systems. The name starts with altr-, as in altruism: when your interest and ours disagree, the decision follows yours.

What that commits us to

4 principles · open any

  1. No sales, no account manager, no subcontractor. The founder is on the first call and on the call at launch.

    What it costs us

    We take fewer projects than a studio with a sales team would.

  2. If a page, a feature or a phase will not earn back what it costs, we say so before it is quoted.

    What it costs us

    The recommendation that removes a feature removes a line from our own invoice.

  3. Estimates are broken down before the contract. A change order arrives before the work starts.

    What it costs us

    A fixed number is a bet against our own estimate, and we pay when it is wrong.

  4. Repositories, domains and pipelines in your name, documented for the engineer after us.

    What it costs us

    We keep no leverage over a client who decides to walk.

Who this is for, and who it isn't.

A good fit
  • A business that needs a system, not a brochure
  • Customers in both English and Arabic
  • Someone who wants to own the code outright
Not a fit
  • A theme with a new logo by Friday
  • A project with no one to make decisions
  • A rebuild meant to lock you in
