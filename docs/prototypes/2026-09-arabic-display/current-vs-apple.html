Read each column top to bottom, then compare across. What changes: Outfit is geometric and round (circles for o, e, a); SF Pro and Inter are grotesques drawn for screens — tighter, more neutral, more "engineered". The Arabic follows its Latin: Vazirmatn is Outfit-friendly, SF Arabic and Noto Sans Arabic are drawn to sit with a grotesque. "Optically matched" scales the Arabic so both scripts read the same size. Column 2 looks right only on your Mac; column 3 is how the same idea ships to everyone.
