Altruvex Sans (Track A) — phase A.2 proof

Production stacks: --stack-prod-en / --stack-prod-ar. Every number on this page is measured in this browser by window.measure() from canvas rasters (ink coverage and ink bounds; the font's own ascent/descent from canvas text metrics). Density = ink pixels ÷ (advance × font size). Ratio = AR density ÷ EN density at the EN weight.

2 · Arabic line height (step 16)

Blue lines mark each line box. Extremes clearance = line height − (lowest ink on line i + highest ink on line i+1), in em, wherever they are along the line: negative means the extremes cross, not that they touch. Contact gap = the smallest white between the two lines in any column where both have ink; overlap = pixels where their ink actually overlaps (red when above zero). Overflow = ink outside the block's first/last line box, in em.
